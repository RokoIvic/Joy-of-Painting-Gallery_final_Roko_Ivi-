About This Site

This website is dedicated to celebrating the life and work of Bob Ross, the beloved painter and television host of The Joy of Painting.

All images of Ross's paintings are sourced from twoinchbrush.com.

This website is made for a university project.

We hope you enjoy exploring the world of Bob Ross and find inspiration in his joyful approach to art.
